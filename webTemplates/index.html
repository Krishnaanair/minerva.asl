WHY MINERVA.ASL?



Minerva. asl is our initiative to lessen the differences between people who are deaf or hard of hearing and society

After a lot of unsuccessful attempts, we have finally brought our Artificially Intelligent model to life. Our AI model converts sign language to speech and text with 99% accuracy.

ISSUES THE DEAF & HARD-OF-HEARING COMMUNITY FACE?



9 in 10 deaf children are born to hearing parents, yet less than a third have family members who sign regularly. Some families rely on the deaf person to read lips, but this is remarkably difficult.

The emotional and psychological toll of not being able to communicate with those closest to you makes it extremely challenging to build relationships.

ASL is more than a gesture-based English translation. It's a different language with its syntax, pronunciation, and word order rules.
